# 🏰 Disneyland Marketing Analytics | Power BI Dashboard

A Power BI dashboard that evaluates marketing campaign performance and visitor
trends using simulated data, to show which campaigns, channels, and time
periods perform best.

![Dashboard Preview](images/dashboard_preview.png)

## 🎯 Objective
To compare campaign results and visitor trends, and identify which campaigns,
channels, and months deliver the highest ticket revenue and return on ad spend.

## 📌 Key Metrics
| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Ticket Revenue | 348.36M |
| Total Ad Spend | 10.38M |
| Total Tickets Sold | 62K |
| ROAS | 33.56 |

## 🔍 Key Insights
- **May is the peak month** (43M), followed by August (39M) and July (38M),
  showing strong summer and spring demand.
- **February is the weakest month** (13M), with a second dip in November (19M).
- **Social Media is the top channel** with 128M in ticket revenue, about
  37% of the total. Email (61M) and Influencer (56M) follow.
- **Early Bird Tickets is the best campaign** by ROAS (about 40.6), while
  Holiday Magic has the lowest (about 27.3).
- Every campaign returns well above its ad spend, so ROAS stays healthy overall.

## 📈 Dashboard Visuals
- KPI cards: Ticket Revenue, Ad Spend, Tickets Sold, ROAS
- Line chart: Total Ticket Revenue by Month
- Bar chart: Ticket Revenue by Marketing Channel
- Donut chart: ROAS by Campaign

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop
- DAX (ROAS = Total Ticket Revenue / Total Ad Spend)
- Excel / CSV (simulated dataset)
